# Square growth log

- 2026-09-21: Players can sit — `C` with empty hands near a bench/sofa/bed/swing toggles sitting (pose `sit`, movement locked, any move stands you up); broadcast over the room so other players see it. Residents already sat/lay at these spots via their job routine's `act:'sit'`.
- 2026-09-21: Eating — `C` with a sandwich or coffee near a café, table or bench eats it (item consumed, brief freeze, `sit` pose) instead of dropping/delivering it. NPCs holding food (`FOOD` in `goose.ts`) now show the eating pose as soon as they stop anywhere in their routine, not only at spots already tagged `act:'eat'`.
- 2026-09-22: Exercise — new pull-up bar and bench press in the park; `C` with empty hands near either exercises for ~2.4s (`pushup`/`pullup` pose, movement locked, sent over the socket). Joggers now route through both spots as part of their job routine, so they exercise on their own the same way.
- 2026-09-22: Watching TV and reading — the TV's spot activity changed from `stand` to a new `watch` (still pose, arms folded); `C` with empty hands near a TV or bookshelf toggles it (movement locked, any move stands you back up), with a "watching"/"reading" line above the head. House owners already stop at the TV and bookshelf on their routine, so they get the same pose and label automatically.
- 2026-09-22: Watering — `C` with empty hands near a garden waters it (`water` pose with drops, ~1.4s, movement locked, sent over the socket). Gardener residents already stopped at `garden`/`garden2` on their routine with `act:'water'`, so they already watered on their own; added task kind `water` (`tasksFor`) and quest kind `water` (`questFor`) so a day's list or a resident's ask can send players to a named bed.
- 2026-09-22: Bins — `C` near a bin with an item consumes it for good (no loose item created, stack shrinks and syncs to others via the normal `pos` update, same pattern as eating). Bin spots (`bin1`-`bin4`) now use the existing `sweep` act instead of `stand`, so the sweeper job (the only job that routes through bins) already empties them with the sweep pose on its own routine. Added task kind `bin` (`tasksFor`) and quest kind `bin` (`questFor`, "take this X and bin it").
- 2026-09-22: Tripping while chasing — a chasing resident rolls a small per-second chance to trip (higher for `retired` and while carrying something): new `trip` pose (`stickman.ts`), drops the carried item, says a flat line, the chase clock loses two seconds, then resumes chasing or gives up. The roll runs before the catch check, so tripping within reach lets the player go, and the escape still counts toward the `chased` task without any extra code. Players running empty-handed at full speed over a loose item on the ground trip the same way (`b.tripped`). Deferred the pond/fountain-edge and broken-prop triggers to a new backlog chain.
- 2026-09-22: Tripping while chasing (chasing-resident half) — a chasing resident now has a per-second chance to trip (retired jobs and anyone carrying something more likely), a new `trip` pose (`stickman.ts`), `Npc.mode = 'trip'` for 1.2s, drops what they carry, says a flat line, and the chase deadline loses two seconds; tripping within reach ends the chase and completes the `chased` task immediately. Broadcast over the room the same way `down` is. Split the pond/fountain-edge, broken-prop and loose-item variants (and the player's own trip-over-a-loose-item-while-running half) into "Grows from Tripping" in the backlog — they touch every moving figure's code, not just the chase block, so they are their own run.
- 2026-09-22: Fishing — `C` with empty hands at a pond takes a rod (new `ItemKey` `rod`; no separate rack prop, the water's edge is the rack); `C` again while holding it casts (`b.fishing`, 4–12s wait, `fish` pose, movement locked); on the bite, a name from `pond.ts`'s `ITEM_LIST` (uniform pick, not the standalone Pond game's zone/rod/bait weighting) shows as a toast and a loose `fish` item drops at your feet through the existing `drop` path, already synced to others (no new `ev` kind). `retired`/`priest` now route through the ponds; `jogger` (and, as a side effect, `dogwalker`, already the only job with a valid route to `pond2`) already did, so they fish there too via the existing act→pose pipeline. Added task kind `fish` and quest kind `pond` ("bring me something from the pond"). Also fixed a merge bug: two duplicate `pose === 'trip'` blocks in `stickman.ts` (from the two tripping runs above) made the second unreachable and failed `tsc`; removed the dead one.
